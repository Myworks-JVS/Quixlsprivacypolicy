Everything You Need for Smarter Quizzes

Quixls is designed to simplify quiz creation, administration, evaluation, and reporting while keeping your quiz data primarily on your device.

Excel-Based Quizzes

Load quiz questions directly from Excel files, making it easy to prepare and manage large question sets.

Interactive MCQ Testing

Present multiple-choice questions in a clean, focused interface designed for efficient testing.

Automatic Scoring

Automatically evaluate responses and calculate results without requiring manual score calculation.

Student Identification

Use Student IDs to identify quiz attempts and organize individual results efficiently.

PDF Reports

Generate professional PDF reports containing quiz results whenever you need a printable or shareable record.

CSV Export

Export quiz results as CSV files for further analysis, record keeping, or use with spreadsheet applications.

Local Data Processing

Quiz content and results are primarily processed locally on your device, helping keep your quiz information under your control.

Image-Based Questions

Support questions containing online images, allowing richer and more visually engaging quiz content.
