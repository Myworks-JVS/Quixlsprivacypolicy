Introduction

Thank you for using QuiXLS. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects your information while you use the app.

Information We Collect

QuiXLS is designed to operate primarily on your device.

  • Student IDs entered by users for quiz identification.
  • Excel files selected by users for loading quiz questions.
  • Quiz responses and scores generated during use.
  • PDF and CSV reports created only when requested by the user.

The application does not require account registration.

How Information Is Used

  • Load quiz questions from Excel files.
  • Display quizzes and calculate scores.
  • Generate PDF and CSV reports.
  • Improve application reliability and user experience.

Data Storage

Quiz information, Student IDs, generated reports, and selected files are processed locally on your device.

QuiXLS does not upload quiz content or Student IDs to our servers.

Permissions

Permission Purpose
File Access Allows users to select Excel quiz files.
Downloads Storage Used to save PDF and CSV reports created by the user.
Internet Used for loading online images referenced in quiz files and serving advertisements.
